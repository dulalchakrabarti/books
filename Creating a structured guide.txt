### Live mobile recording workflow for GenZ farmer demos

You’re building something that matters—clear, auditable steps GenZ farmers can follow and insurers can trust. Below is a modular, reproducible workflow you can use to record live, step‑by‑step demos on a phone: geotagging crop images, installing and activating Gemini voice, getting a voice‑driven table of crop details, and saving the recording for claims.

---

## Capture setup and screen recording

- **Device prep:**  
  - **Battery & storage:** Ensure >30% battery and at least 2–3 GB free space.  
  - **Network:** Stable 4G/5G or Wi‑Fi for voice and uploads.  
  - **Location services:** Turn on device Location (GPS) for geotagging.

- **Screen recording (Android/iOS):**  
  - **Android:** Use the built‑in Screen Recorder from Quick Settings; enable microphone audio to capture your narration.  
  - **iOS:** Use Control Center → Screen Recording; long‑press to enable microphone audio.  
  - **Framing:** Record in portrait for app flows; switch to landscape only if maps or tables need width.  
  - **Privacy:** Hide personal notifications (Do Not Disturb) and avoid capturing unrelated apps.

- **On‑camera segments (optional):**  
  - If demonstrating field context, switch to rear camera briefly, then return to screen recording.

> **Tip:** Gemini Live supports camera and screen sharing—use it to narrate while showing the app flow if available on your device and region.

---

## Geotagging crop images (field capture)

- **Enable geotagging:**  
  - **Camera app settings:** Turn on “Save location” or “Geotag photos.”  
  - **Maps check:** Open Maps to confirm GPS lock before capturing.

- **Capture protocol:**  
  - **Angles:** Take 3–5 images—wide plot view, mid‑range canopy, close‑up leaf/fruit.  
  - **Context shot:** Include a landmark or plot marker for visual verification.  
  - **Narration:** State farmer ID/plot ID, crop type, date/time, and village/district while recording.

- **Verification:**  
  - **Gallery/Photos:** Open the image details to confirm location metadata (lat/long) is present.  
  - **Fallback:** If geotag fails, record a short clip with Maps showing your blue dot at the plot, then retake photos.

> **Note:** Some OEM camera apps or privacy modes disable geotagging—always verify metadata immediately after capture.

---

## Gemini app installation and voice activation

- **Install:**  
  - Open the app store, search “Gemini,” install, and sign in with a Google account.  
  - Grant microphone and camera permissions for voice and image features.

- **Activate voice:**  
  - In Gemini, enable voice input and test a short prompt (“What’s the weather?”).  
  - If available, try Gemini Live with camera/screen sharing to demonstrate real‑time guidance.

- **Image + voice flow (optional):**  
  - Capture or select crop images and ask Gemini to analyze them—many tutorials show integrating camera capture and content extraction with Gemini’s vision capabilities.

> **Reminder:** Availability of Gemini Live features varies by device/region; if screen sharing isn’t present, proceed with standard voice prompts.

## Voice prompt to tabular response (crop type, growth stage, health)

- **Prompt design:**  
  - **Context:** “You are assisting a GenZ farmer. Analyze the provided crop images and my voice notes.”  
  - **Structure request:** “Return a table with columns: Crop type, Growth stage, Health status, Key indicators, Confidence (0–1).”  
  - **Constraints:** “Use concise entries; avoid speculative claims; cite visible indicators.”

- **Example voice prompt (spoken while recording):**  
  - “Analyze these geotagged images of the plot. Based on leaf shape, canopy density, and visible panicles, identify the crop type, estimate growth stage, and assess health. Return a table with columns: Crop type, Growth stage, Health status, Key indicators, Confidence. If uncertain, mark ‘Needs field verification’ and state why.”

- **Follow‑ups:**  
  - “Add a second row for suspected nutrient deficiency and list visible symptoms.”  
  - “Include a short note on recommended next observation within 7 days.”

- **Capture the output:**  
  - Record the on‑screen table response.  
  - If the app returns text, scroll slowly and read it aloud for the recording.

> **Gemini Live can combine camera and screen sharing for richer demos; use it when available to show both the field view and the table response. Tutorials also demonstrate image capture and analysis flows with Gemini’s vision features.

## Save, name, and package the recording for insurance claims

- **File naming (auditable):**  
  - Format: `YYYYMMDD_FarmerID_PlotID_Crop_Device_GPS-Verified.mp4`  
  - Example: `20260115_F123_P07_Rice_Pixel7_GPS-Verified.mp4`

- **Metadata bundle:**  
  - **Primary:** The screen recording MP4.  
  - **Evidence:** Original geotagged images (JPEG/HEIC), a screenshot of image location details, and a short Maps clip showing the plot location.  
  - **Transcript:** Export or manually note the voice prompts and the table response.  
  - **Checklist:** A one‑page PDF with steps completed and timestamps.

- **Integrity & privacy:**  
  - Keep farmer consent on record (audio at the start: “Consent recorded on [date] for documentation and claim submission”).  
  - Avoid capturing unrelated personal data; blur if needed before submission.

- **Submission:**  
  - Upload via the project portal or insurer’s app; verify successful upload and retain local + cloud backups.

## Reproducible checklist (use during recording)

1. **Location on; geotag enabled; GPS lock confirmed.**  
2. **Record start; microphone on; DND enabled.**  
3. **Capture 3–5 crop images; verify geotags.**  
4. **Open Gemini; voice activated; permissions granted.**  
5. **Speak structured prompt; request tabular output.**  
6. **Record the table; read key fields aloud.**  
7. **Save MP4 with auditable filename.**  
8. **Bundle images, location screenshots, transcript, checklist.**  
9. **Upload; confirm receipt; backup.**
